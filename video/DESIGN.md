# Medical AI Teacher — Video Design

## Style Prompt
Premium clinical education: calm, intelligent, modern, and trustworthy. Use a dark clinical canvas with restrained teal and cyan accents, white typography, subtle medical-grid texture, clean cards, and purposeful motion. Visuals should teach the mechanism rather than decorate it. Keep the screen uncluttered and prioritize the clinical clue, answer logic, distractors, exam trap, and memory anchor.

## Colors
- `#07111F` — clinical dark canvas
- `#0E2A36` — secondary panel
- `#19C3B1` — primary clinical accent
- `#8BE9E0` — secondary highlight
- `#F7FAFC` — primary text

## Typography
- Inter — primary Latin/UI type
- Noto Sans Arabic — Arabic captions and dialogue

## Motion
- Calm, precise entrances.
- Use short fades, upward reveals, and diagram-path animations.
- No frantic zooms or excessive kinetic typography.
- Emphasize trigger words and answer logic with controlled highlights.

## What NOT to Do
- No generic corporate blue template.
- No distracting stock footage.
- No tiny medical text.
- No rapid cuts that make Arabic/English captions hard to follow.
- No decorative animation that competes with the clinical explanation.
