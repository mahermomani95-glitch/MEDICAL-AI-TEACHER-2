# Medical AI Teacher — Video Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
Premium medical education: cinematic but restrained, clinical clarity, dark navy canvas, white typography, cyan clinical accents, warm amber emphasis for exam traps, clean 2D medical diagrams, subtle depth, generous spacing, no visual clutter. The viewer should feel like they are watching a high-end medical teaching session rather than a generic AI slideshow.

## Colors
- #07111F — deep navy background
- #10243D — secondary clinical panel
- #F5F8FC — primary text
- #55D6E8 — clinical accent
- #FFB454 — exam-trap / warning accent

## Typography
- Inter — English UI and captions
- Noto Sans Arabic — Arabic captions and narration text

## What NOT to Do
- No generic corporate stock-video aesthetic.
- No excessive gradients, glow, or particle effects.
- No tiny medical text that is unreadable on mobile.
- No unsupported clinical diagrams or claims.
- No rapid transitions that reduce comprehension.

## Motion
Use restrained cinematic motion: 0.5–0.8s entrances, short crossfades, deliberate emphasis pulses, and enough hold time for medical terminology to be read and understood.
